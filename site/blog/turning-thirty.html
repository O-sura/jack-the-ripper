Thirty

14 March 2022

I turn thirty today, which apparently means I am now allowed to have opinions about mattresses.

Thirty years, and the ones I would keep are mostly unremarkable: a bad first job that taught me everything, a move I did not want to make, and a dog I was talked into.

My mother rang at seven this morning to tell me what time I was born, which she does every year, and every year I pretend I did not already know it.

No party. A long ride out past Kaduwela, something fried on the way home, and an early night like a sensible old man.
