Ten years out of Greenfield

2 May 2021

A few of us from the Greenfield College batch got together up in Kandy last month — the first time since we left in 2009. Same jokes. Worse knees.

I was there from year one, which means Greenfield had me for eleven years and somehow still let me graduate. Mr. Weerasinghe's maths class is the only reason I can do this job, and I have never told him that.

Strange thing about a school reunion: everyone reverts. Ten minutes in and we were all fourteen again, sitting in the same order.
